## The situation

An externally operated Asterisk system suffered recurring failures affecting internal lines.
Responsibility was blurred: the organization depended on the platform but did not control its
lifecycle, configuration or recovery path.

## Investigation

The incident work exposed a deeper problem than a single outage. The platform had remained
substantially outdated for approximately eight years, and operational knowledge was concentrated
outside the organization.

## Recovery

I recovered and modernized the PBX, documented the operating model and moved responsibility
in-house. The goal was not a theatrical rewrite. It was to restore reliability, ownership and a
credible path for future change.

## Result

The recovered system remained in successful operation for approximately three more years and the
organization no longer depended on the original supplier for routine control.
