## The situation

A growing call-centre operation needed its first internally controlled IP telephony platform.
The system had to support daily business traffic while the organization expanded from one office
to three geographically separate locations.

## My responsibility

I designed, deployed and operated the Asterisk/Trixbox environment, including Linux servers, SIP
gateways, endpoints, routing behavior and connectivity between sites. The work combined
architecture with hands-on incident diagnosis; a clean diagram is pleasant, but a failed RTP path
still wants someone who understands packets.

## Scale

- approximately 45 active call-centre users;
- three operating locations;
- three to five Linux servers;
- up to ten SIP gateways;
- SIP signaling, RTP media, routing and endpoint interoperability.

## Result

The platform supported the organization's expansion and gave the internal team direct operational
control over a business-critical communication system. It also established the telephony and
Linux foundation used in later PBX recovery and integration work.

The figures are based on retained professional documentation and are presented as approximate
operational scale rather than synthetic precision.
